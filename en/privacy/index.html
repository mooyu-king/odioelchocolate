Website analytics

We use the analytics service at data.1back.link to understand visits and interactions. Requests may include the visited page, referrer and ordinary network or browser information. We do not send form contents or the chocolate checklist as event properties.

Data stored on your device

The tracker uses localStorage to remember each mode. sessionStorage avoids repeating a session event. The Service Worker may cache public website pages and assets for later visits. You can clear these in browser settings; your local checklist will be lost.

External game and video

Starting the game connects you to our s.odioelchocolate.games subdomain, hosted on Cloudflare Workers and R2. Your browser may cache game resources; we do not send the manual chocolate checklist to the game. Thumbnails are requested from YouTube; playback opens youtube-nocookie.com. These services may process technical data under their own policies. Their resources are not cached as part of this app.

Email and requests

The Android form prepares an email; it does not automatically send or publish your data. If you send it, we receive your address and message through the email provider. We use that information to respond and manage the issue; you may request access or deletion by contacting us. We retain what is necessary to handle the request and any applicable obligations.
