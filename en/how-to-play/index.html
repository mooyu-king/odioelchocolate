THREE THINGS TO WATCH

Read the HUD before you run

01

Top left: objective

The fraction shows that run’s progress. Check whether its denominator is 3, 13 or another value before using a guide. A 3/13 image does not reveal the locations of the remaining ten chocolates.

02

Top right: inventory

Screenshots show three slots and a selected-item label. “el poor” also appears: we do not interpret it as currency or invent a shop. Choose a slot and check its label before using it.

03

Bottom area: stamina

The bar helps you manage movement; it is not a victory timer. Alternate movement and observation while practicing. We do not publish a recharge time or consumption formula without measurements.

A DECISION-BASED GUIDE

From the first doorway to the exit attempt

This staged walkthrough organizes your decisions. It does not claim that the map or objects always appear in the same places. If the run changes, keep the method and adapt your route to what you see.

01

1 · Calibrate your first attempt

The menu recommends Low Cortisol for newcomers. Spend the opening moments walking, turning and opening a door. If camera control feels difficult, adjust the settings offered by the game before turning practice into a long search. There is no universal sensitivity that suits everyone.

02

2 · Complete each room’s loop

Note your entrance, inspect the room and check the counter after attempting a pickup. Then orient yourself toward the doorway again. If you find nothing, decide whether the room is checked or needs another visit; avoid turning a half-finished inspection into repeated identical visits.

03

3 · Decide before turning

At a junction, choose your next area and remember the previous one. If you lose orientation, return to a known landmark rather than chaining random turns. During an encounter, avoid reading this guide while holding a movement key: pause or release the pointer first.

04

4 · Count, then follow the exit objective

In Main, reaching 13 is part of the objective, but the menu also asks you to get out. Check what changes on screen and follow your run’s instructions. Do not restart just because our manual checklist is full: it cannot detect completion or activate doors.

CHANGE ONE THING AT A TIME

When a run gets stuck

I click, but nothing is collected

Restore focus inside the game. Move a little closer and put the target in front of you; try left-clicking again. Watch for the number to increase. Do not refresh the page as your first fix: you may lose the run already in progress.

I keep revisiting the same corridor

Use a stable reference—a door color, a row of lockers or a junction—and distinguish “checked” from “walked through.” Screenshot scenery illustrates the idea but does not establish a compulsory room order.

I use an item by accident

Separate selection from use: first 1–3, then check the label, then right-click. A screenshot showing “el Soda” establishes a selected label, not its effect duration. Practice the controls before relying on an item to escape an encounter.

A video shows a different result

Compare platform, mode name and visible target. An Android run, PC run and browser conversion are not interchangeable just because they share a title. Use videos as context; do not transfer a shortcut, bug or item position without checking your edition.

A LITTLE HELP

Good questions.

01Is this the official website?

No. This is an independent fan site. The original Windows and Android releases are on Wantho’s itch.io profile.

02Will it work on my phone?

The web edition includes a joystick, swipe turning and touch buttons. These have been tested in browser emulation, but not yet on physical Android phones. The Android edition is a separate download and the author warns of issues on some devices.

03Why is loading taking a while?

The game downloads external resources when started. A slow network, limited memory or browser restrictions can stop loading. Try the direct link or check the original downloads.

04How many chocolates do I collect?

Images show counters of 3 and 13. Targets can change by stage; reaching 3/3 does not establish completion. Follow your edition’s counter and exit instructions. This site’s checklist is manual and adjustable.

05Can I play offline after installing the site?

No. Installation creates a shortcut and allows website content to be cached; the external game and videos still require Internet.
